Our collaboration with projects is built on trust, openness, and mutual respect. This also means being thoughtful about how we communicate our partnerships publicly. Rather than featuring individual projects on our website, we focus on our overall approach and the areas of work we support.

In 2026, we support over 25 projects, organisations, and initiatives in Germany, across Europe, and around the world. Their work spans a wide range of issues, from democratic participation and fighting discrimination to fostering intercultural exchange and creating spaces for connection, as well as promoting a free and open internet and a just digital transformation. What brings these efforts together is a shared commitment to a fairer, more open, and more democratic society. We provide the people doing this work with the infrastructure they need to make it happen.
